How it works

Buy a track or album with USDC. We verify the payment on-chain, then send download links.

  1. Browse the shop and add a track or the full album.
  2. Enter your email (for delivery) and pay in USDC on Base with a wallet, or paste a transaction hash.
  3. Archonia Verify verifies the transfer. You get download links on the order page and by email.

Each file link works up to 5 times and expires after 7 days — enough for retries. Save the files locally.

Payments are verified by Archonia Verify — the buyer payment goes 100% to the band wallet.
